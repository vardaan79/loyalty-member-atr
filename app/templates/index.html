Method & caveats

Member definition: a customer mobile on any bill containing a loyalty card article (…018 Silver / …003 Gold / …004 Platinum); member sales counted from first card purchase date. POS bills carry no loyalty card ID (LOYALTYCARDID empty), so this is the best available proxy.
Windowing: sale-line analytics (mix, GM, member behaviour) are filtered to the selected window; member identity (card since, tier = highest card SKU ever held) stays lifetime; enrolment trend and card revenue are windowed; campaign dormancy anchors on the window end date.
Tier: Silver ₹200 / 2% slab · Gold ₹500 / 5% · Platinum ₹1,000 / 11%.
Non-member: all other bills in the same window (incl. walk-ins without mobile and members' purchases before enrolment).
Sales: Axapta POS lines (CRM_BRONZE.AXAPTA_SALESTRANS), sign-flipped, net of returns, card articles excluded; zero-value lines (carry bags, freebies) excluded from quantity.
GM: unit cost from GOLD.LOYALTY_ART_COST (store × article actuals), fallback LOYALTY_ART_COST_ANY then VW_PRODUCT_MASTER.ARTICLE_COST; uncosted articles excluded from GM maths (coverage shown). Costs are ex-tax landed, sales MRP-inclusive → GM% reads rich vs books. Slab-discount estimate = |line disc| where |disc ÷ (price×qty) − tier rate| < 0.5pp.
Employee factor: active employees from HRMS tblEmployee (mobile-normalised); Axapta staff-discount mechanism = ACX_EMPID / ACX_EMPDISCAMOUNT on the bill. "Double-dip" = staff-discount bill whose payer mobile also holds a membership card.
Campaign lists: CSVs under reports/targets/ ranked by member-period spend; 30-day dormancy; upgrade thresholds ₹25k / ₹50k; suppression list = employee mobiles.
